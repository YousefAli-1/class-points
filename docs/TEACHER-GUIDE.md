# Class Points: Teacher Guide

Everything saves by itself as you tap. There is no Save button.

## Getting started on the iPad

1. Open **Safari** and go to the app's address (it ends in `.workers.dev` unless you have your own domain).
2. Type your password and tap **Log in**. You stay logged in for two months on each device.
3. Tap the **Share** button (the square with an arrow), then **Add to Home Screen**. Now the app opens from its own green icon, full screen, like any other app.

Your laptop and phone work the same way: open the address and log in. Changes you make on one show up on the others.

## Your daily routine

1. Open the app. The calendar shows today with a yellow ring.
2. Tap **today** (or one of the subject buttons next to the calendar).
3. Pick the **subject** at the top (for example 📚 English).
4. Tap **▶ Start lesson**. Every child starts the lesson with a star.
5. During the lesson, tap the cards on a child's tile:
   - **Warning** gives a red card. Tap it again to take it back.
   - **Penalty** gives a second red card. It only works after a warning.
6. That's it. Pick the next subject when it starts.

Started a lesson by mistake? Tap **⋯** at the bottom and **Cancel this lesson**.

## What the colours and points mean

| On the tile | Points (you can change these) |
|---|---|
| No cards, green ⭐ badge | **+1** |
| Warning (one red card), yellow badge | **0** |
| Warning + Penalty (two red cards), coral badge | **−1** |
| Absent (grey tile, 🏠) | no points, and it doesn't count against them |

The bar at the bottom adds up the lesson: stars, warnings, penalties and absences.

The top bar tells you about saving: **All saved ✓** (green) means everything is safe. **Offline: will save when back** (yellow) means the internet dropped: keep going, it catches up by itself.

## Absent children

Tap the **🏠** in the top-left corner of the child's tile. The tile turns grey and the cards switch off. Tap 🏠 again if they arrive.

## Reports and printing

Tap **📊 Reports**. Choose **Day**, **Week** or **Month** and use the arrows to move through time. The chips pick one subject or all of them. Tap a child's row to see their own report. Tap **Print** to print it or save it as a PDF.

## Admin and your PIN

Tap **🔒 Admin** to change students, subjects, points, school days, holidays, your password and more. It asks for your **PIN**.

- **Unlock admin while the iPad is not on the board**, so the class doesn't see your PIN. Stop mirroring, unlock, then mirror again.
- Admin locks itself after 10 minutes, or tap **Lock** when you're done.
- If the class shouldn't see the reports on the board, turn on **Reports need PIN** in Admin → Account.
- The 🔊 button in the top bar mutes the sounds on that device.

Forgot your password or PIN? Tap **Forgot password?** on the login page and use your **setup code** (the code on the paper you were given).

## Backups

Your data is stored safely online, and the last 7 days can always be rolled back if something goes wrong.

Once a month, go to **Admin → Backup** and tap **Download backup**. Keep the file (for example in your email or a USB stick). The app reminds you when it has been more than 30 days.
